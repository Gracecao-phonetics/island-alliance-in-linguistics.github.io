The Island Alliance in Linguistics and Languages (IALL) is a global research network dedicated to connecting scholars, educators, and community resource builders based on islands or working with island languages. Islands are vibrant epicenters of linguistic diversity, home to thousands of unique languages, distinct dialects, and rich oral traditions. Yet, researchers working in these regions often face geographic isolation, limited institutional resources, and distinct sociolinguistic challenges, from language endangerment to resource scarcity.

IALL bridges these geographic divides by fostering interdisciplinary collaboration, sharing field methodologies, and promoting open-access language resources across island nations and archipelagos. Whether you focus on Irish and Celtic dialectology in the North Atlantic, Austronesian language structures across the Indo-Pacific, Creole dynamics in the Caribbean, or computational tools for low-resource island languages, this network serves as your collaborative hub.

By unifying isolated research efforts into a collective voice, IALL empowers local and global scholars to safeguard, document, and celebrate the world's island linguistic heritage. Join us in shaping a connected future for island language research.
