Established in 2026, the Island Alliance in Linguistics and Languages (IALL) is a global research network dedicated to connecting scholars, educators, and community resource builders based on islands or working with island languages. Founded by linguists from two institutes in different islands, University College Dublin in Ireland and National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University in Taiwan, we aim to build a stronger collaborative network on topics like language endangerment, sociolinguistic diversity, heritage language preservation, Creole languages, multilingualism and language variation and change via IALL. 

While researchers working in islands/working with island languages often have limited institutional resources, IALL bridges these geographic divides by fostering interdisciplinary collaboration, sharing field methodologies, and promoting open-access language resources across island nations and archipelagos. Whether you focus on Irish and Celtic dialectology in the North Atlantic, Austronesian language structures across the Indo-Pacific, Creole dynamics in the Caribbean, or computational tools for low-resource island languages, this network serves as your collaborative hub.

By unifying isolated research efforts into a collective voice, IALL empowers local and global scholars to safeguard, document, and celebrate the world's island linguistic heritage. Join us in shaping a connected future for island language research.
